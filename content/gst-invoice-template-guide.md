## Overview (main "Content" field)

A GST invoice is the document a registered business issues for a taxable sale of goods
or services in India. It isn't just a bill — under GST law it must contain specific
details, or your buyer can be denied their input tax credit (ITC) and you can face
penalties. This template gives you a compliant format that does the tax maths for you.

The template is built for **intra-state sales** (a sale within the same state), where
GST splits into **CGST** and **SGST** at half the GST rate each. For example, an 18%
GST item carries 9% CGST + 9% SGST. Enter the quantity, rate, and GST rate for each
item, and the sheet calculates the taxable value, both taxes, and the grand total for
you. (For **inter-state** sales — to a different state — you instead charge a single
**IGST** at the full rate; see the FAQ below.)

## What fields a GST invoice must legally include

Indian GST law (Rule 46 of the CGST Rules) requires these on a tax invoice. This
template already has a place for each:

1. **Supplier details** — your business name, address, and GSTIN.
2. **Invoice number** — a consecutive, unique serial number (max 16 characters) for the
   financial year. Don't repeat or skip numbers.
3. **Invoice date.**
4. **Customer details** — name, address, and (if registered) their GSTIN.
5. **Place of supply** — the state where the goods/services are delivered. This decides
   whether you charge CGST+SGST or IGST.
6. **HSN or SAC code** — the classification code for each good (HSN) or service (SAC).
7. **Description, quantity, and rate** for each item.
8. **Taxable value** and any discount.
9. **Tax rate and tax amount**, split into CGST/SGST or IGST.
10. **Whether GST is payable on reverse charge.**
11. **Signature** of the supplier or an authorised signatory.

## How to use the template (the "Instructions" steps)

1. **Download the file** and open it in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice.
2. **Fill in your business details** — replace every `[ ]` placeholder in the top-left
   block (company name, address, GSTIN, state).
3. **Add the invoice number, date, and place of supply** in the box on the right.
4. **Enter your customer's details** in the "Bill To" (and "Ship To" if different) box.
5. **Add your items** — for each row, type the description, HSN/SAC, quantity, rate, and
   GST rate. The taxable value, CGST, SGST, and total fill in automatically.
6. **Check the Grand Total**, write the amount in words, add your bank details, and sign.
7. **Save as PDF** before sending, so the buyer can't accidentally change the figures.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Charging CGST+SGST on an inter-state sale** (or IGST on a local one). The *place of
  supply* vs your state decides which applies — get this wrong and the invoice is
  incorrect.
- **Skipping or repeating invoice numbers.** They must be a clean, consecutive series.
- **Missing the HSN/SAC code.** Most businesses are required to print it.
- **Forgetting the place of supply** — it's a mandatory field, not optional.

## FAQs (for the "FAQ" section)

**Is this GST invoice template free?**
Yes. It's free to download and edit for personal and business use.

**Does it work in Google Sheets?**
Yes. Upload the file to Google Sheets; the formulas carry over. Some formatting may
look slightly different, but the calculations work the same.

**How do I make an inter-state (IGST) invoice?**
For a sale to a different state, you charge one IGST at the full GST rate instead of
splitting into CGST + SGST. You can rename one tax column to "IGST" and change its
formula to `=Taxable × GST%` (the full rate). We also publish a separate IGST version.

**What GST rate should I use?**
It depends on the item — common slabs are 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Check the official
GST rate for your specific goods or services. The template works with any rate you enter.

**Can I use this to claim input tax credit?**
Your *buyer* claims ITC using a valid tax invoice. As long as all mandatory fields are
filled correctly, this template produces a compliant invoice that supports their claim.
